# Artwork, architecture, and audio provenance

The current medieval and Roman scenes use original procedural geometry from [`site/landmarks.js`](../landmarks.js) and [`site/world.js`](../world.js). Their castles, villages, temples, ports, people, and vegetation are assembled in code. The scene does not load the retained Kenney GLBs below. Original decorative SVG emblems live in [`site/presentation.js`](../presentation.js); no published board-game artwork or rulebook scans are bundled.

## Generated illustrations in use

These original illustrations were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project:

The portraits are decorative identities, not asymmetric character cards. `kingdom-cover.png` is the earlier title treatment and the source for the renamed cover and text-free panorama; its original prompt remains in [`kingdom-cover-prompt.txt`](kingdom-cover-prompt.txt). It and the older `site/og.png` remain in the repository but are no longer metadata images. Generated artwork is separate from the CC0 model packs; the Kenney licenses apply only to those packs.

| `models/tower.glb` | Skyscraper A / Commercial |
| `models/tower-twin.glb` | Skyscraper E / Commercial |

Both packs are **CC0**. Their original palette textures were embedded into self-contained GLBs; geometry and colors were unchanged. Original notices are preserved in [`LICENSE-kenney-commercial.txt`](LICENSE-kenney-commercial.txt) and [`LICENSE-kenney-industrial.txt`](LICENSE-kenney-industrial.txt).

## Sound and narration

[`site/audio.js`](../audio.js) synthesizes an original 72-second coastal arrangement and six tabletop SFX cues using Web Audio. Plucked strings, flute, harmony, and surf are generated in code; no music recordings, downloaded samples, or reference melodies are included. Playback starts only after a user gesture and follows device-local preferences.

Field-guide read-aloud uses the browser's speech synthesis service, preferring an available local English voice and otherwise using its default. No prerecorded narration or third-party voice asset is bundled; voice availability depends on the browser and device.
